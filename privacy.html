2Data We Collect

  • Identity & Contact: name, email, phone number, business name

3How We Use Your Data

  • Service Delivery: confirmations, reminders, and review requests

  • Marketing (optional): promotional content only if you've opted in

4SMS Messaging (TCPA Compliance)

  • Opt-In: You may opt in to receive SMS when submitting a form, via an unchecked consent box
  • Content: confirmations, reminders, review requests, and optional promotional messages
  • Frequency: varies depending on your activity
  • Charges: message and data rates may apply
  • Opt-Out: reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time
  • Help: reply HELP or email support@cleansignal.org
Consent is not a condition of purchase.

5Legal Basis / Consent

We process your data to deliver the services you request, meet legal obligations, and — when appropriate — based on your explicit consent (e.g., for marketing).

6Sharing

We only share data with trusted vendors required to operate CleanSignal:

  • GoHighLevel (CRM/workflows)
  • Twilio (SMS delivery)
  • Stripe (payment processing)
  • Web/email hosting providers

We do not sell your personal data.

7Data Retention

We retain data while your account is active, and for legal or business requirements thereafter. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

8Security

We use transport encryption (HTTPS), access restrictions, and vendor due diligence to protect your data. However, no method is 100% secure.

9Your Rights

You may request to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct or update it
  • Delete your account
  • Withdraw consent for messaging (STOP/HELP)

Contact us at support@cleansignal.org

CleanSignal is not intended for use by children under 13. If you are under 13, do not submit personal information.
